# Northstar Labs Employee Handbook

> **Demo data only.** Northstar Labs is a fictional company. This handbook is a polished sample dataset for testing and demonstrating the RAG application; it is not legal, HR, or financial advice.

- **Document ID:** NSL-HR-2026
- **Effective date:** 1 January 2026
- **Owner:** People Operations
- **Review cycle:** Annual

## 1. Parental and Maternity Leave

Eligible employees may take up to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave or parental leave following birth, adoption, or placement of a child. The benefit applies from the employee's first day, including during a probationary period. Employees should notify People Operations at least 30 days before foreseeable leave. When 30 days' notice is not possible, notice must be given as soon as reasonably practical.

Contractors and agency workers are not covered by the employee leave benefit. Their leave entitlement is controlled by their signed service agreement and applicable law.

## 2. Business Travel and Expenses

Employees traveling within their home country may claim lodging up to USD 180 per night and meals up to USD 75 per day. International travel permits lodging up to USD 250 per night and meals up to USD 100 per day. All international travel requires written manager approval before booking.

Contractors may claim travel only when the expense is expressly authorized in their statement of work. Reimbursement uses the lower of the documented actual cost or the limit written in that agreement. Every traveler must submit itemized receipts within 15 calendar days after returning.

## 3. Remote Work

Full-time and part-time employees who have completed 30 days of service may request up to three remote-work days per week. The employee must have satisfactory performance, a manager-approved schedule, and a workspace that meets security requirements. Roles that require continuous on-site access may be excluded.

Contractors may work remotely only when both their company sponsor and service agreement permit it. Interns may request one remote-work day per week after completing their first 30 days.

## 4. Annual Leave and Probation

Full-time employees receive 20 paid annual-leave days per calendar year, prorated for partial years. Leave accrues during probation, but an employee may normally begin using it after 90 days of service. A manager may approve earlier use for urgent circumstances. Up to five unused days carry into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March.

Part-time employees receive prorated annual leave according to their contracted weekly hours. Contractors do not accrue employee annual leave.

## 5. VPN Access and Account Recovery

To reset VPN access, open the identity portal at `https://identity.northstar.example/reset`, select **VPN access**, and complete multifactor identity verification. The service desk sends a new device-registration link after verification. If the portal is unavailable or the employee has lost every registered factor, contact the service desk and provide the employee ID; support will verify identity before restoring access.

VPN credentials must never be shared. The verification step protects company systems when a password, device, or authentication factor is lost or compromised.

## 6. Information Security and Data Handling

Confidential company information may be stored only in approved systems. Employees and contractors must report suspected disclosure, malware, or lost devices to Security Operations within one hour of discovery. Customer data must not be copied to personal storage, personal email, or unapproved AI services.

Access is granted according to job need and reviewed every quarter. People leaving the company lose access at the end of their final working day unless Security Operations approves an earlier cutoff.
